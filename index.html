The person behind the letters

Hi, I'm Amanda.

I'm not a guru, and this isn't an agency. I'm a founder — Texas-raised, building businesses of my own, surrounded by women doing the same thing at kitchen tables all over the country.

She's Onto Something started with two friends: a home bakery six years in, and a farm account with a chicken named after a pop star. I looked closely at what they'd built, did the research they never had time to do, and wrote each of them a letter about what I saw. Watching them read those letters is why this business exists.

I believe the women building things deserve to be seen before they're sold to. So that's the whole model: I look first. I write you something real. What happens next is up to you.

— Amanda
